Ordering Policies

Please read before you order ♡

Minimum Order

At least 6 per category: 6 cupcakes, 6 brownies or 6 mini cakes. Mix flavors within a category (regular and loaded brownies count together).

Categories can't be combined to reach the minimum. 3 cupcakes + 3 brownies is not an order.

Payment

Orders under $75 are paid in full.

Custom orders and orders $75 or more need a 50% non-refundable deposit. The balance is due at pickup.

Confirmation

Your order is confirmed only after your payment is verified. Until then, your date isn't held.

  1. Received
  2. Pending payment
  3. Confirmed

You'll get a confirmation with your order details, payment status and pickup info, plus a reminder the day before pickup.

Timing

Please order at least 3 days ahead. Rush orders may have a rush fee, and Mone'T will confirm your final price before you pay.

Deposits are non-refundable.
